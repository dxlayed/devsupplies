FAQ

Which coins can I deposit?

The coins listed on the Deposit tab of your account. Send to the address shown, then submit the transaction ID.

How long does a deposit take?

Deposits are checked by hand, usually within a few hours. Your balance updates as soon as it's approved.

Is anything a subscription?

No. Every product is a one-time purchase.

Something's wrong with my order

Get in touch with the order number from your Orders tab and it'll be sorted out.
