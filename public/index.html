Questions

Still not sure? Here are the things people ask most.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. Waurks runs on our servers, so it keeps working when your computer is off.

Why do you need my Roblox cookie?

It's how we accept and decline friend requests for you. We store it encrypted and only use it for that. You can remove it from your account page whenever you want, and changing your Roblox password logs it out too.

What if it declines someone I know?

If we're not sure about an account, we don't decline it. It goes in a list on your account page so you can pick yourself.

How do I pay?

Litecoin (LTC) or in-game items. With Litecoin your key shows up by itself a few minutes after you pay.

What happens when my key runs out?

Waurks stops checking your requests until you get a new key. Lifetime keys never run out.
