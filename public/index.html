FAQ

Questions

Does it decline the bots on Roblox?

No. Bots are just filtered out on the site so you never see them. Once a bot is filtered it stays filtered.

Do I need to give my Roblox cookie?

No. The Waurks browser extension uses the Roblox tab you already have open. You connect it by signing in, no key or cookie to paste.

What is the MM2 filter?

It only lets through players who have the MM2 Level 10 badge, so you only get real traders.

How do I pay?

Litecoin (LTC). Your key shows up by itself a few minutes after you pay.

What happens when my key runs out?

Waurks stops checking until you get a new key. Lifetime keys never run out.
