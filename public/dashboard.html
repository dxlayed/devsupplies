Finding your Roblox cookie

  1. Go to roblox.com on a computer and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click the page and pick Inspect).
  3. Click the Application tab (it's called Storage in Firefox). On the left, open Cookies and click https://www.roblox.com.
  4. Find .ROBLOSECURITY, copy its value and paste it here.

Only paste this into Waurks. Anyone who has this cookie can get into your Roblox account. If you ever want to cancel it everywhere, change your Roblox password.
