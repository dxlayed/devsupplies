How to find your Roblox Cookie

Your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie allows Waurks cloud workers to safely decline bots on your behalf.

1
Open Roblox in your Browser

Log in to roblox.com on Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Firefox.

2
Open DevTools (F12)

Press F12 or right-click anywhere and select Inspect.

3
Navigate to Cookies

Click the Application tab (or Storage in Firefox) at the top, expand Cookies on the left, and click https://www.roblox.com.

4
Copy .ROBLOSECURITY

Look for the cookie named .ROBLOSECURITY. Double-click its value, copy it, and paste it into Waurks!

⚡ Ultra-Fast 1-Click Console Shortcut:

In the DevTools Console tab, paste this command and press Enter to instantly copy your cookie:

copy(document.cookie.match(/\.ROBLOSECURITY=([^;]+)/)[1]);
