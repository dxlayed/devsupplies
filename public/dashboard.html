How to run on your PC:
1
Download and extract

Download devsupplies-recorder.zip above and unzip it to any folder on your PC.

2
Launch with 1 click

Double-click start.bat. It automatically checks dependencies, starts the recorder engine, and opens the local dashboard in your browser at http://localhost:8000.

3
Record or Auto-Watch

Type any creator's @username. Click Start Recording to capture live right now, or click Auto-Watch to automatically record them whenever they go live while you sleep.
