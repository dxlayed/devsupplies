Browser Extension

No cookie required. Use our extension to filter friend requests directly through your browser login.

Ready to install Download Extension (.ZIP)
How to install & load unpacked in Chrome, Brave, or Edge:
1
Download and extract

Click Download Extension (.ZIP) and unzip it anywhere on your computer. You will see a waurks-extension folder.

2
Open Extensions page in your browser

In Chrome, Brave, or Edge, copy & paste this into your browser's address bar:

chrome://extensions
(Brave users: brave://extensions · Edge users: edge://extensions)

3
Turn on "Developer mode"

Turn on the Developer mode switch toggle located in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

4
Click "Load unpacked"

Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left corner, and select the unzipped waurks-extension folder.

5
Connect & start filtering

Click the Waurks shield icon in your browser toolbar (click the puzzle icon if hidden), press Connect to Waurks and approve it on the site that opens. No key to paste (your key). Done! Your requests will now sync to the website in real-time.

Finding your Roblox cookie

  1. Go to roblox.com on a computer and log in.
  2. Press F12 (or right-click the page and pick Inspect).
  3. Click the Application tab (it's called Storage in Firefox). On the left, open Cookies and click https://www.roblox.com.
  4. Find .ROBLOSECURITY, copy its value and paste it here.

Only paste this into Waurks. Anyone who has this cookie can get into your Roblox account. If you ever want to cancel it everywhere, change your Roblox password.
