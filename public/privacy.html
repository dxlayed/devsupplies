Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Core Security Guarantee

Waurks ("we", "our") is designed exclusively to detect and filter automated bot friend requests on Roblox. We believe privacy should be provable by architecture:

  • Zero Cookie Access: The Waurks browser extension manifest does not request the cookies permission in Chrome/Edge/Brave. The browser sandbox physically forbids it from reading, storing, or transmitting your .ROBLOSECURITY cookie.
  • Zero Password or Credential Collection: We never prompt for, collect, or store your Roblox password or login credentials.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We never sell or share user data with advertising networks or data brokers.

2. Data Transmitted to Waurks

When you sync your pending friend requests using the extension or cloud interface, only the minimum data required to score automated bots is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our servers:

  • Roblox Account Info: Your Roblox User ID and username (to associate friend requests with your account).
  • Pending Friend Request Metadata: The sender's public User ID, username, display name, account creation date, bio description (truncated to 300 characters), verified badge status, and mutual friends list.
  • Badge Counts: Public badge counts (numbers only, e.g. whether the sender has played Murder Mystery 2 or Adopt Me) to power game-specific filters.
  • Device Identifier: A randomly generated client token (ext-<uuid>) stored in your browser's local storage to pair your browser extension with your Waurks license key.

3. Data We NEVER Collect

  • Your Roblox password, PIN, or two-factor authentication codes.
  • Your Roblox session cookies (.ROBLOSECURITY).
  • Your credit card or bank numbers (subscriptions are paid peer-to-peer using Litecoin).
  • Your web browsing history, bookmarks, or activity outside roblox.com.

4. How Data Is Used & Retention

All transmitted request metadata is analyzed strictly for bot indicators (e.g., account age, suspicious scam bios, botnet flood timing, and badge counts). The server returns only the final action (accept or decline):

  • Pending Requests: Retained in our secure SQLite database while actively pending or filtered so you can review them in your dashboard.
  • Action Nonces & Sync Tokens: Ext tokens and license bindings are retained during the duration of your active key.
  • Data Deletion: Disconnecting your account from the dashboard or extension immediately revokes and deletes the associated browser session token.

5. Security Architecture

All communication with Waurks occurs strictly over transport-layer security (HTTPS). Server endpoints authenticate all requests via signed tokens and rate-limit syncing to prevent abuse.
